# Third-party materials

Prime-argument workshop (`primes.html`):

Euclid, _Euclidis Elementa_, volume II, _Libros V–IX continens_, edited and
translated into Latin by J. L. Heiberg, Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1884. The title
prints MDCCCLXXXIV. Public-domain historical text and images from the University
of Toronto digitized copy: https://archive.org/details/euclidisoperaomn02eucluoft.
The title and printed pp.184–185, 250–253 and 388–391 are retained as eleven
whole unretouched reductions. Original PDF and derivative identities are in
`public/sources/primes/manifest.json`; reproduction is in `scripts/primes-sources.py`.
IX.20 is selected in full; VII definitions 1–2 are complete; VII.31 is represented
by selected clauses beside its complete printed proof. New English, language
notes, examples, algorithms, code and diagrams are MIT. The existing Noto Serif
and full SIL OFL notice are reused in portable readings.

Solid-corner workshop (`solids.html`):

Euclid, _Euclidis Elementa_, volume IV, _Libros XI–XIII continens_, edited and
translated into Latin by J. L. Heiberg, Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1885.
Public-domain historical text, Latin and page images. Internet Archive copy:
https://archive.org/details/euclidisoperaomn04eucl.
The title and printed pp.4–5, 54–59 and 336–341 are retained as fifteen complete
reductions without cropping or retouching. The original PDF identity and each
JPEG's bytes and SHA-256 are in `public/sources/solids/manifest.json`; the
reproduction script is `scripts/solids-sources.py`. New English, language notes,
coordinate comparisons, code and paper diagrams are MIT. The room distinguishes
the ancient excerpts, Heiberg's editorial note and its modern mathematical
explanations. It reuses the bundled Noto Serif and full SIL OFL notice.

Book V ratios workshop (`ratios.html`):

Euclid, _Euclidis opera omnia, Elementa_, volume II, _Libros V–IX continens_,
edited and translated into Latin by J. L. Heiberg, Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1884.
Public domain. University of Toronto, Gerstein Science Information Centre copy,
digitized by Internet Archive:
https://archive.org/details/euclidisoperaomn02eucluoft.
The title and printed pp.2–5 are complete reductions without cropping or
retouching. Their identities and original PDF are pinned in
`public/sources/ratios/manifest.json`. Greek, Latin and scans remain public domain;
new English, contextual notes, examples, code and diagrams are MIT. Heiberg’s
algebraic footnotes are editorial explanations, distinguished from Euclid’s text.
The existing Noto Serif and complete OFL notice are reused in portable readings.

Greek text, Latin translation and source page images:

Euclid, _Euclidis opera omnia_, vol. I, ed. and Latin translation J. L. Heiberg,
Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1883. Public domain. Scan contributed by the Gerstein
Science Information Centre, University of Toronto, and hosted by the Internet
Archive: https://archive.org/details/euclidisoperaomn01eucluoft

MIT license does not impose new restrictions on these public-domain materials.

Book X workshop (`measure.html`):

Euclid, _Euclidis opera omnia_, vol. III, _Elementa, liber X_, edited and
translated into Latin by J. L. Heiberg, Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1886, pp.2–13.
Public domain. Google digitization of the University of Michigan copy,
distributed at https://www.wilbourhall.org/pdfs/HeibergEuclidVolume_3.pdf.
Full pages retain scanned handwriting, the critical apparatus and the Google
watermark. No textual image has been cropped or retouched. PDF and page
identities, rendering command and transcription conventions are recorded in
`public/sources/measure/manifest.json`. New English, grammar notes, diagrams
and the diagonal specialization are original teaching work under MIT.

Archimedes circle workshop (`circle.html`):

Archimedes, _Archimedis opera omnia cum commentariis Eutocii_, vol. I,
ed. and Latin translation J. L. Heiberg, Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, **1880**,
pp.262–271. Ancient Greek, Heiberg's translation and printed page images:
public domain. Digitized volume distributed at
https://www.wilbourhall.org/pdfs/Archimedes_VOL_I.pdf.

Ten whole-page JPEG reductions retain diagrams, apparatus and editorial notes;
no image is cropped or retouched. `public/sources/circle/manifest.json`
identifies the original PDF, one-based page mapping and every retained image.
The new English, word notes, proof exposition, exact inequality audit, code and
drawings are original teaching work under MIT. Printed numerical values are
not silently converted into exact equalities; transcription choices and the
retained Latin/Greek discrepancy are explained in `docs/circle-model.md`.
The existing locally bundled Noto Serif and complete OFL notice are reused.

Euclid horn-angle workshop (`horn.html`):

Six complete 1883 volume-I pages, pp.208–213, from the same Gerstein/Toronto
Internet Archive scan identified above. Original PDF and whole-page identities
are retained in `public/sources/horn/manifest.json`. The eight selected readings
preserve the Greek corollary addition in editorial brackets and distinguish
Heiberg’s Latin footnote from a parallel translation. Ancient text, Heiberg’s
Latin and printed pages are public domain. New English, word notes, coordinate
witness, diagrams, magnification and code are original teaching work under MIT.

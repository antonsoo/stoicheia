# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-21

- Full Greek, Heiberg Latin and English reading passages for Euclid I.1–3, with
  26 aligned steps and contextual notes.
- Calculated construction plates with established equalities, source-grounded
  reasons, changed givens, alternate orientations and invalid-hypothesis handling.
- Selected Greek/Latin vocabulary, complete reading, source-page inspection and
  zoom, shareable reading state, SVG and standalone study-sheet exports.
- A locally bundled font and eight printed source pages, with an offline
  production edition and explicit transcription provenance.
- Independent high-precision circle-intersection verification and browser checks.

## Continued course · 2026-10-21

  an explicitly hypothetical contradiction diagram, preserved in downloads.

## Foundations and reading practice · 2026-10-21

- Thirteen definitions, postulates and common notions in all three languages,
  linked directly from the reasons that use them. Preserve Heiberg’s numbering,
  editorial brackets and omissions; disclose the cropped source margin on p. 6.
- Six additional source scans, making twenty-two pages available offline.
- Greek-first and Latin-first reading with translations revealed on demand.
- Twelve passage exercises with explanations of each interpretation and direct
  return to the corresponding proof step.
- Standalone study sheets now include each step’s reason and the relevant
  foundation texts, so the argument can be followed away from the app.
